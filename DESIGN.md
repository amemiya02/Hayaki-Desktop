# Design System: Hayaki Desktop

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Native Desktop, Tinted"**

Hayaki Desktop is built to sit among the platform's own best apps (Apple Notes, Finder, Windows 11 Settings): a source-list sidebar tinted with the current Theme and, where the OS draws one, letting the window backdrop through; an opaque white or near-black work area; system UI fonts at desktop density. It is neither a phone layout stretched across a monitor nor Anki's Qt-and-web patchwork, and it refuses the grey SaaS sidebar with one blue button. The Theme accent is the only voice the frame has; everything else is a neutral role derived from it and the Appearance.

The study screen is where the system pays off. Starting a session collapses the sidebar and toolbar in one 200ms move into a 44px strip on the Theme canvas, with a 2px accent line draining as Cards are answered; Escape restores the shell. The Card then leads, rendered on its own origin, never restyled by the interface, and the next Card is pre-rendered so an answer lands in under 120ms. Everything around it (counts, answer buttons, menus) stays small, flat and quiet.

Every colour is a role, never a literal, across ten Themes and three resolved Appearances (Light, Dark, Black). The tokens above are the Hayaki Theme in Light unless suffixed `-dark` or `-black`; Black reuses Dark's accents and signals over true black. The other Themes swap only the accent pair and, for Paper and Midnight, the study canvas. The window keeps the native OS caption on purpose, so Windows Snap Layouts keep working.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Theme-tinted sidebar (accent mixed 7% into a cool neutral in Light, 6% in Dark, 5% in Black); over Mica on Windows 11 and vibrancy on macOS it becomes a translucent tint. The work area is always opaque.
- A sidebar the person can collapse (Ctrl+\) and resize (200 to 380px).
- One filled accent button per screen state; selection, focus rings, the progress line and pressed tools carry the accent.
- Anki's blue, red and green appear only on new, learning and due counts, the Card-state charts and the four answers; flags, marked, suspended and buried keep Anki's own colours.
- Flat at rest; one shadow (`shadow-pop`) for things that float: menus, popovers, tooltips, dialogs, the command palette, the shortcuts sheet, toasts.
- Grouped containers with 12px corners on screens and 10px inset groups in dialogs; controls at 7 to 10px corners.
- Hierarchy by size and weight in the system face, on an eight-step scale; tabular figures for every number.

## Colors

One Theme accent over cool, nearly hue-less neutrals, with Anki's signal and marker colours held to their meanings.

### Primary
- **Hayaki Teal** (`accent`, `accent-dark`): the Theme accent. Fills the one primary button, the profile avatar, the Switch track when on, the study progress line and focus rings (2px outline, 2px offset). Each of the ten Themes (`theme-*`) supplies a Light accent that reaches 4.5:1 on its ground and a Dark accent that Black reuses.
- **Accent Ink** (`accent-ink`): the accent mixed 88% with black in Light (the accent itself in Dark). Text and icons on accent-tinted ground: the active nav item, the selected Theme name, filtered Deck names, pressed tools, checkmarks, CodeMirror keywords and field references.
- **Accent Hover** (`accent-hover`): the accent with 10% ink in Light, 12% white in Dark. The primary button's hover.
- **Accent Soft / Softer** (accent at 13% / 7% over transparent in Light, 24% / 9% in Dark): the selected nav item, highlighted menu and palette items, pressed tool-rail buttons, the 3px field focus halo; Softer marks keyboard-focused Deck rows, drop targets and the editor's active line. **Selection** is the accent at 22% (32% in Dark).

### Secondary
- **Anki Signals** (`count-new` blue, `count-learn` red, `count-due` green, `mature` deep green; `answer-again` / `-hard` / `-good` / `-easy`): fixed across every Theme, lighter values in Dark and Black. Counts above zero wear their colour on a `--tint-pill` pill; the queue bar draws new, learning, due in that order.
- **Statistics Series** (`stat-relearn`, `stat-filtered`): relearning is the learning red mixed 50% toward the surface in Light, and reviews in filtered Decks are ink at 26% over the surface. In Dark and Black both are fixed values (`stat-relearn-dark`, `stat-filtered-dark`) held at 3:1 or more on the panels, because mixing toward a dark surface drops under 3:1.
- **Markers** (`marked` indigo, `suspended` yellow, `buried` grey): Anki's Note and Card markers. The study strip's star, Browse's row tints and the Browse filter icons. They never borrow answer or flag colours.
- **Flags** (`flag-red` to `flag-purple`, with Dark values): Anki's seven flags, as `--flag-1` to `--flag-7`. Flag icons, and on Browse rows a `--tint-row` tint plus a 3px stripe.

### Neutral
- **Ground** (`bg`, `surface`, `raised`): white in Light; a `bg-dark` work area with `surface-dark` groups and `raised-dark` popovers in Dark; true black in Black.
- **Recessed** (`surface-2`, `surface-3`): table header rows, the toolbar and sheet search fields, Kbd keys, hover fills, dialog inset groups, the Segmented track, stepper hovers and the calendar's empty cells.
- **Hairlines** (`line`, `line-strong`, `line-soft`): every border; `line-strong` on hover and as the off Switch track; `line-soft` (line at 60%) divides rows in the Deck table, the profile list and the shortcuts sheet.
- **Ink** (`ink`, `ink-2`, `ink-3`): primary text, secondary text and icons, tertiary text and zero counts.
- **Knob** (`knob`): the Switch knob, white in Light and `#f4f5f6` in Dark.
- **Sidebar Pane** (`sidebar-pane`, the sidebar tint 60% over `bg`): Browse's filter column.
- **Study Canvas** (`canvas`): the Card's ground. White / `#17191a` by default, warm paper or cool blue for the Paper and Midnight Themes, black in Black.
- **Danger** (`danger`, `on-danger`): destructive menu items, the danger button and its text, field errors.

### Tint strengths
One tint strength per job, as tokens: `--tint-pill` 11% (14% in Dark) for count pills, `--tint-row` 13% (16% in Dark) for marked, suspended, buried and flag rows, `--tint-halo` 18% for the danger focus halo.

### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** Each screen state has at most one filled accent button (Study on Decks, Study Now on a Deck, Show Answer in Study, Save in Deck Options, Add in Add, Open in the profile chooser). Dialogs carry their own single primary. The toolbar's Import and Add are secondary.

**The Anki Signal Rule.** Blue, red and green mean new, learning and due; the four answer colours mean the four answers; flag and marker colours mean flags and markers. Card states never take the Theme accent, so they read the same in every Theme.

**The Earned Pill Rule.** A count wears its colour, weight 600 and a tinted pill only when it is above zero. A zero is `ink-3` at weight 400 with no pill.

**The Role Not Hex Rule.** Surfaces read roles from `src/styles/app.css`; Themes change only the accent pair and canvas. A new surface writes no hex value. The only literal colours in components are content palettes the person picks from (editor text and highlight colours, Image Occlusion mask fills) and the Theme swatches, which preview other Themes.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Segoe UI Variable Display (with Segoe UI, SF Pro Display, Helvetica Neue)
**Body Font:** Segoe UI Variable Text (with Segoe UI, SF Pro Text, Helvetica Neue); captions use Segoe UI Variable Small.
**CJK:** chosen per interface language through `:lang()`: Microsoft YaHei UI / PingFang SC for Simplified Chinese, Microsoft JhengHei UI / PingFang TC for Traditional, Yu Gothic UI / Hiragino Sans for Japanese, Malgun Gothic / Apple SD Gothic Neo for Korean, each with Noto Sans CJK and Source Han Sans after it.
**Code Font:** Cascadia Mono (with SF Mono, Consolas) for templates, CSS and other code-like values.

**Character:** the platform's own UI face at desktop size, with Windows 11's optical sizes riding on the scale, so the app reads as native rather than branded. The root is 14px at line-height 1.45; most controls set 13px.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, 28px, 1.2, Display optical size): the Deck Overview title only.
- **Headline** (600, 22px, 1.3, Display optical size): the Decks summary sentence, the Finished screen and the profile chooser.
- **Heading** (600, 18px, 1.35): empty states, the error screen, section heads in Add.
- **Title** (600, 15px, 1.4): the toolbar screen title, dialog and sheet titles, the command palette input, Show Answer.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 1.5): Settings row labels, Deck descriptions (max 62ch), command palette items, answer labels (600), large buttons.
- **Control** (500, 13px, 1.45): buttons, nav items, menu items, fields, Segmented options, counts.
- **Caption** (400 to 600, 12px, 1.45, Small optical size): hints, legends, tooltips, menu shortcuts, intervals, sidebar group labels (600 `ink-2`), dialog section labels.
- **Micro** (500, 11px, 1.35, Small optical size): Kbd keys, the answer key row, the avatar initial.

### Named Rules
**The Size-and-Weight Rule.** Hierarchy comes from size and weight (400, 500, 600) only. Nothing is italic, set in capitals or letter-spaced for emphasis, because Chinese and Japanese have no italics or capitals to fall back on.

**The Matched Semibold Rule.** Microsoft YaHei UI and JhengHei UI ship Regular and Bold only, so on Windows with Chinese the semibold step is 660, not 600, and Han glyphs and Latin digits carry the same colour. Yu Gothic UI and the macOS faces keep 600.

**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Every count, interval, date and version number uses tabular figures, so columns of numbers never jitter.

## Layout

A two-column window grid: the sidebar and a flexible work area (`<width>px minmax(0,1fr)`), with a 52px toolbar (sidebar toggle, screen title, a 260px search field with a `/` key hint, Import, Add) over the screen. The window opens at 1280 by 820 with a minimum of 880 by 600.

The sidebar opens at 248px and the person resizes it from 200 to 380px by a 7px separator on its edge, whose 1px line turns accent on hover, focus and drag; the arrow keys move it 8px (32px with Shift), Home and End jump to the limits, Enter or a double-click resets it. Ctrl+\ or the toolbar's panel button collapses it; the column animates to 0 over 200ms on `--ease-out` (instant while dragging) and the hidden sidebar becomes inert. In Focus (Study) the same move runs and the toolbar row goes to `0fr`.

Screens centre a content column: Decks at 1080px, Deck Overview at 760px, Settings at 720px, Deck Options at 1000px with a 176px section rail from a 960px container, Statistics at 1440px with two panel columns from a 1000px container. Page padding is 32px sideways, 28 to 40px on top. Browse is a three-pane workspace: a 220px filter column on `sidebar-pane`, a dense table with 32px rows and an editor pane. Add puts a 340px preview panel beside the editor. The Image Occlusion editor puts a 48px vertical tool rail beside its canvas.

Spacing follows a 4px grid: 4 and 8px inside controls, 10px sidebar insets, 12 to 16px row padding, 20px panel and dialog padding, 24px between page sections, 32px page gutters. Row heights are fixed per role: 30px sidebar rows, 44px top-level Deck rows (40px nested), 52px minimum Settings rows, 44px minimum dialog switch rows, 36px palette and shortcut rows, 32px table and menu rows. Study answer buttons sit in a 60px band at most 640px wide, with their keys 1 to 4 always laid out beneath.

Screens settle in over 140ms (`--dur-quick`, `--ease-out`, opacity and a 4px rise) when the route changes. Study has no route transition (it has the Focus move), and nothing moves between Cards.

### Named Rules
**The 520 Rule.** The queue bar (new, learning, due) is drawn in one order and colours and is never wider than 520px.

**The Still Card Rule.** Nothing in the study layout changes size between question and answer; the answer band and key row are reserved from the start.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default, with tonal layering: sidebar tint, `bg` work area, `surface` groups and `surface-2` recessed rows, separated by hairlines rather than shadows. One shadow exists for things that float above the page.

### Window material
The shell asks the OS for a backdrop (`windowEffects` Mica and sidebar, a transparent window, `macOSPrivateApi` with the `macos-private-api` feature) and the interface confirms it before showing it, by marking the document `data-material`:
- **Windows 11** (`userAgentData` platform version 13 or more): Mica. The window ground goes transparent and the sidebar surface becomes the accent mixed 7% into a translucent neutral (`rgb(242 244 245 / 0.55)` Light, `rgb(28 30 32 / 0.45)` Dark), opaque enough that `ink-2` labels keep 4.5:1 over Mica's lightest and darkest renderings.
- **macOS**: sidebar vibrancy, the same translucent sidebar. Enabled in code but not yet verified on a Mac.
- **Windows 10, Linux and the Black Appearance**: opaque; the sidebar is the plain `sidebar` tint.

The work area is always opaque `bg`; only the sidebar shows the backdrop.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Pop** (`shadow-pop`, values in the sidecar): menus, sub-menus, popovers, tooltips, dialogs, the command palette, the shortcuts sheet, editor tooltips and toasts.
- **Thumb** (`shadow-thumb`): the selected Segmented option and the Image Occlusion colour dot. Darker in Dark.
- **Knob** (`shadow-knob`): the Switch knob.
- **Ring** (`shadow-ring`, 3px `accent-soft`) and **Ring Danger** (`shadow-ring-danger`, 3px danger at `--tint-halo`): field focus halos, with the border turning accent or danger.
- **Ring Inset** (`shadow-ring-inset`, inset 2px accent): the focus ring inside rows and list items, where an outline would be clipped.
- **Scrim** (`rgb(0 0 0 / 0.28)`, Dark `0.5`): behind dialogs and sheets.

### Named Rules
**The One Step Rule.** A surface is either on the page (flat, hairline) or floating (Pop). There is no in-between elevation for cards, panels or buttons; Thumb and Knob are physical cues on control thumbs only.

**The Opaque Work Area Rule.** The OS backdrop shows through the sidebar and nowhere else. The work area, the study canvas and every floating layer are opaque.

## Shapes

Gently rounded and consistent by scale: 5px for Kbd keys and the Deck-tree chevron, 6px for menu items, tooltips, NumberField steppers and the focus ring, 7px for small buttons, nav and sidebar rows, palette items and Segmented options, 8px for standard buttons, fields, tool-rail buttons and Theme swatches, 9px for the Segmented track, 10px for large buttons, menus, popovers, dialog inset groups, the code editor and toasts, 12px for grouped containers, Statistics panels, the Card preview frame and the answer buttons, 14px for dialogs, the command palette and the shortcuts sheet. Counts, the queue bar, the progress dots, the Switch, colour swatches and the avatar are fully round. Borders are 1px hairlines everywhere; there are no outlines thicker than the 2px focus ring.

## Components

### Buttons
Quiet and compact, native in proportion.
- **Shape:** 8px corners at the standard 32px height (7px at 28px small, 10px at 40px large; large sets 14px text).
- **Primary:** accent fill, `on-accent` text, 13px / 500, 14px side padding; hover goes to `accent-hover`.
- **Secondary (default):** `surface` with a `line` border; hover goes to `surface-2` and `line-strong`.
- **Ghost:** `ink-2` text, no border; hover fills with the sidebar hover tint and turns `ink`.
- **Danger:** `danger` fill with `on-danger` text; hover drops to 90% opacity.
- **Press / disabled:** every button scales to 0.98 on press over 90ms (`--ease-standard`); disabled is 45% opacity.
- **Icon Button:** a square ghost button (28, 32 or 40px) with a Radix tooltip after 500ms naming the action and its shortcut.
- **Kbd:** an 18px `surface-2` key with a hairline and 11px `ink-3` text; inside a primary button it becomes `on-accent` at 18% with no border.

### Chips (Count Pills)
- **Style:** 22px tall, at least 30px wide, fully round, 13px tabular. Above zero: weight 600, Anki colour text on the same colour at `--tint-pill`. Zero: `ink-3`, weight 400, no fill.
- **State:** in the study strip the queue the current Card came from takes the `--tint-pill` fill; counts change in place, without motion.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 12px on screens; 10px for inset groups inside dialogs.
- **Background:** `surface` on `bg`; dialog groups are `surface-2` on `raised`.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see Elevation).
- **Border:** 1px `line`; rows inside divided by `line` (Settings, dialogs) or `line-soft` (Decks, profiles).
- **Internal Padding:** 16px rows in Settings-style lists (labels at 14px), 14px rows in dialog groups (labels at 13px), 20px in panels. Group headings sit outside the container: 13px / 600 `ink-2` on screens, 12px / 600 `ink-2` in dialogs.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 32px, 8px corners, `surface` with a `line` border, 13px text, `ink-3` placeholder, accent caret; hover turns the border `line-strong`. The toolbar and sheet searches sit on `surface-2` with a transparent border.
- **Focus:** the border turns accent with the 3px Ring halo, no outline.
- **Error / Disabled:** `aria-invalid` turns the border `danger` with Ring Danger; disabled is 45% opacity.
- **NumberField:** the one number control, a spin box like Anki's. Right-aligned tabular figures, 124px wide (136px for percentages, with a `%` suffix), and two 24px chevron steppers at its end (6px corners, `ink-3`, hover `surface-3`). No native spin arrows anywhere. Typing commits on blur or Enter, clamped to range; arrows and steppers move one step, Shift or Page keys ten.
- **Switch:** 38 by 22px, accent track when on, `line-strong` when off, a 16px `knob` with the Knob shadow sliding over 200ms.
- **Segmented:** a `surface-3` track (9px, 3px inset) of 28px options (24px with 12px text at `sm`); `stretch` makes the options share the full width. The selected option lifts to `raised` with the Thumb shadow.

### Navigation
- **Sidebar:** Theme tint (or the material surface), 10px inset. Library items (Decks, Browse, Statistics) are 30px rows with 16px icons, 13px / 500; hover takes the sidebar hover tint, the current item `accent-soft` with `accent-ink` text. Group labels are 12px / 600 `ink-2`, sentence case. The Deck tree indents 14px per level, rotates its chevron over 200ms, opens and closes children by height, and shows the due total in 12px tabular `ink-2`. A hairline footer holds the profile menu (24px accent avatar, name, up-down chevron), Sync and Settings.
- **Toolbar:** 52px, hairline bottom, the sidebar toggle, a 15px / 600 title, the search field (hidden on Browse), Import and Add; hidden in Study.
- **Menus:** `raised`, 10px corners, 4px inset, Pop shadow, 140ms pop-in (scale 0.97, rise 2px); items 32px with `accent-soft` highlight and right-aligned 12px shortcuts.

### Dialogs
440px by default, 14px corners, `raised` with a hairline and Pop, a 200ms scale-in from 0.96 over the scrim. 15px / 600 title, 13px `ink-2` description, a small close button, right-aligned footer buttons with one primary. Settings inside follow one pattern: a 12px / 600 section label over a 10px `surface-2` inset group with divided rows, Switch rows at least 44px, and groups that expand by height (200ms, opacity 140ms) when a Switch opens them.
- **Initial focus:** an explicit `autoFocus` or `data-autofocus` wins, then the first field, then the footer's last (primary) button; content that is still loading holds focus on the dialog itself. Never the close button. Confirmations put `data-autofocus` on the safe choice.

### Command Palette (Ctrl+K)
Every destination, Deck and tool, reachable by typing. 600px wide, 14vh from the top, 14px corners, `raised` with Pop over the scrim. A 48px input at 15px under a search icon, a hairline, then 36px items (7px corners, 14px text, 16px `ink-2` icons, `accent-soft` when selected, shortcuts in 12px `ink-3`) grouped under 12px / 600 `ink-3` headings.

### Profile Chooser
A full-window `bg` screen with a centred 440px column that rises in over 320ms: a 48px mark, a 22px headline, a 12px group container of profiles divided by `line-soft` (the selected name in `accent-ink`), then Open as the one primary with an Enter key. ## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** read colours from the role tokens (`accent`, `surface`, `line`, `ink-2`, `count-due`, `marked`, `flag-1`) and let Themes swap only the accent pair and canvas.
- **Do** set text on the scale (`text-micro` 11, `text-caption` 12, `text-control` 13, `text-body` 14, `text-title` 15, `text-heading` 18, `text-headline` 22, `text-display` 28) so the optical sizes follow.
- **Do** take shadows and tints from their tokens (`shadow-ring`, `shadow-ring-danger`, `shadow-ring-inset`, `shadow-thumb`, `shadow-knob`, `--tint-pill`, `--tint-row`, `--tint-halo`).
- **Do** keep one filled accent button per screen state and make everything else secondary, ghost or icon.
- **Do** draw today's queue as new, learning, due in blue, red, green, at most 520px wide, and Card states in Statistics as new, learning, relearning, young, mature, then suspended and buried.
- **Do** use tabular figures for every number and fixed row heights (30, 32, 36, 44px) so columns hold.
- **Do** use `--dur-fast` (90ms) for hover and press, `--dur-quick` (140ms) for menus, fades and route settles, `--dur` (200ms) for layout moves and dialogs, `--dur-slow` (320ms) for screen entrances and bars, `--ease-out` for entrances and `--ease-standard` for state changes; read them in Motion through `motionTokens()`, and let reduced motion make every motion instant.
- **Do** use the shared `NumberField`, `Switch`, `Segmented` and `Dialog` from `components/ui.tsx`.
- **Do** open dialogs on their first control, and mark the safe choice with `data-autofocus` in confirmations.
- **Do** keep base element rules (html, body, selection, focus ring, caret, scrollbars) inside `@layer base` so Tailwind utilities override them.
- **Do** show every action's Anki shortcut, in tooltips, menus and Kbd keys.

### Don't:
- **Don't** restyle Card Content or move it into the interface's origin; it lives in the card page.
- **Don't** give Card states, counts or answers the Theme accent, or use Anki's signal, flag or marker colours to decorate.
- **Don't** add a second filled primary to a screen, or a shadow to a flat surface; only floating layers take Pop.
- **Don't** let the OS backdrop show through the work area, the canvas or a floating layer.
- **Don't** replace the native window caption with a custom title bar; it would lose Windows Snap Layouts.
- **Don't** use native number spin arrows.
- **Don't** put a dialog's initial focus on its close button.
- **Don't** make hierarchy with italics, capitals or letter-spacing; use size and weight.
- **Don't** write hex values in a screen.
